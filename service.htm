Poojas, Homams & Ceremonies

We offer the following services, performed as laid down in the scriptures.

We conduct poojas, homams and ceremonies offered in worship of the Divine to seek blessings and grace. Larger events with additional priests can also be arranged on request. Don't see what you need? Contact us with your occasion and preferred date and we will let you know if we can arrange it.

We do not conduct weddings, as customs differ from one community to another, or ancestral rituals.

🪷 Divine support in times of need 🪷

Pay-With-Heart Pooja

Financial hardship should never stand between a family and divine blessings.

Are you, or someone you know, going through a difficult phase in life? We offer traditional Vedic poojas for families facing genuine hardship or financial distress, with prayers tailored to bring peace, strength and resolution.

Prayers offered for

  • Health and healing: serious illness, chronic pain or mental distress
  • Employment and stability: sudden job loss, financial stress or career blocks
  • Family harmony: restoring peace and resolving relationship issues at home
  • Obstacle removal: clearing hardships and inviting positive energy into the home

Our promise to you

  • Pay only what you can comfortably afford. Give from the heart; there is no fixed charge.
  • Strict confidentiality. Your situation stays completely private.
  • Authentic rituals. Performed with the same devotion, care and tradition as every ceremony.

If you know a neighbour, friend or relative who could benefit from this support, please let them know, or contact us directly.
