About us

Serving Singapore's Hindu families since 2009

Over the years we have conducted poojas and homams for many families across the island.

Our approach: every prayer we conduct is performed exactly as laid down in the scriptures. These procedures were given by the Rishis for the benefit of all beings, and we follow them without compromise. This steadfastness, together with the host's sincerity and commitment in gathering the required items, and the right intention of both the host and the priest, is what brings the full blessings and merits of the prayer.

Our vision

A Sanatana Dharma community that helps everyone, regardless of caste, creed, race, religion or nationality.

Our mission

To guide each family through their prayers, and along the way to teach them what truly matters, so that whoever they engage in future, they will know what a proper prayer requires. In this way the original teachings are passed on to the younger generation, preserved unchanged, and every family continues to receive the full merits of their prayers.

Many families perform poojas and homams regularly, yet feel they have not received their full blessings. Often the reason is simple: the ritual was not done as laid down in the scriptures.

We believe the answer is to return to authentic rituals, performed correctly and with the right intention.

From the scriptures

एकं सद्विप्रा बहुधा वदन्ति

ekaṁ sad viprā bahudhā vadanti

“God is one; the wise call Him by many names.”

Rig Veda 1.164.46

The Rig Veda is the oldest of the four Vedas, Hinduism's most sacred scriptures, and one of the oldest texts in the world.

In this spirit, it is the duty of every Hindu to respect every other faith as their own. Whether one prays to Ganesha, Shiva, Jesus, Mother Mary, Lord Buddha, Allah, Kuan Yin or Tua Pek Kong, we see the same one Divine.

सत्यमेव जयते“Truth alone triumphs.”Mundaka Upanishad
