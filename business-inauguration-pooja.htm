Business Inauguration Pooja in Singapore

A business inauguration pooja seeks divine blessings when opening a new shop, office, clinic or restaurant, so that the venture begins on an auspicious note and prospers.

When is it performed?

This pooja is performed on the opening day of a new business or premises, or when moving to a new office. It is held at an auspicious time.

What the ceremony includes

  • Ganapati Pooja for a smooth start
  • Purification of the premises
  • Mahalakshmi Pooja for prosperity
  • Blessings for the owners and staff

How we conduct it

  • Performed strictly according to the Hindu Agamas and Shastras
  • Mantras chanted in Sanskrit, with explanations in English and other Indian languages
  • Conducted at your own business premises
  • Registered in Singapore since 2009

Common questions

Can the pooja be done before opening hours?

Yes. The pooja can be held before opening hours, at the auspicious time you have chosen.
