Community Service

Serving the community: beyond the homes we visit, we organise events where the wider community can come together in prayer.

Faces have been blurred to protect privacy.

With the blessings of the former grassroots advisor for Kebun Baru, we held a two-day community Navarathri celebration in Ang Mo Kio. Families of different races and from all walks of life came together to worship the Divine Mother and take part in the event.

On the eve of the event, essential groceries were delivered personally to families in need in the neighbourhood, and they were invited to join us at the venue the next day for a meal. On that day, we were happy that our grassroots advisor joined us for the prayers, and he was kind enough to accept our request to present a token of appreciation to these families.

Later, a two-day Sahasra Modaka Sri Maha Ganapati Homam was held on Vinayaka Shashti (Pillayar Nonbu), another significant day for the worship of Sri Ganapati, made possible by a group of like-minded well-wishers. Seniors from a home were invited to join us and take part in the event.

दातव्यमिति यद्दानं दीयतेऽनुपकारिणे ।
देशे काले च पात्रे च तद्दानं सात्त्विकं स्मृतम् ॥

dātavyam iti yad dānaṁ dīyate ’nupakāriṇe
deśe kāle ca pātre ca tad dānaṁ sāttvikaṁ smṛtam

“A gift given because it is right to give, to one who cannot give anything in return, at the right place and time and to a worthy person, is known as pure (sāttvic) giving.”

Bhagavad Gita 17.20

In keeping with the above sloka, at both events, those who contributed in cash or kind, and those who oversaw the arrangements, did not seek any recognition or a place for themselves. Though each of them carried their own prayers in their hearts, they chose not to be included in the sankalpam. Instead, the guests and walk-in devotees were welcomed in, the sankalpam was made in their names, and the prasadam was given to them first.

God does not look at who sits in front, or whose name is called in the sankalpam. He sees the quiet intention of those who step aside so that others may receive His blessings. In time, each of them received what they needed most at that point in their lives. All glory be to God.

These events were offered to the community with devotion, and not for profit.
