About us

Serving Singapore's Hindu families since 2009

For nearly two decades, families across our island have engaged us for poojas and homams in their homes. Every ritual follows the scriptures given by our Rishis, and families feel its positive effects in their lives and homes. Many engage us again for subsequent prayers in their homes, and also recommend us to their relatives and friends.

Our approach: the rituals and procedures of every pooja and homam we conduct will not be altered or simplified. They are strictly followed as laid down in the scriptures, and we are firm on this. On the other hand, if a family has constraints in gathering some of the prayer items, please tell us openly and we will suggest a respectful alternative (lotus-leaf plates, yes; plastic plates, no). Your sincerity in preparing, and the right intention of both host and priest, bring the full merits of the prayer.

Many families tell us they have hosted prayers before but could not feel their effects. We will not comment on the reasons. Sri Rajarajeswari Divine Services was founded in 2009 for exactly this: to perform the poojas and homams that invoke God's grace properly, without cutting short any step. Engage us once and experience it for yourself.

Our vision

A Sanatana Dharma community that helps everyone, regardless of caste, creed, race, religion or nationality.

Our mission

To guide each family through their prayers, and along the way to teach them what truly matters, so that whoever they engage in future, they will know what a proper prayer requires. In this way, the original teachings are passed on, unchanged, to the younger generation, and every family continues to receive the full merits of their prayers.

From the scriptures

एकं सद्विप्रा बहुधा वदन्ति

ekaṁ sad viprā bahudhā vadanti

“God is one; the wise call Him by many names.”

Rig Veda 1.164.46

The Rig Veda is the oldest of the four Vedas, Hinduism's most sacred scriptures, and one of the oldest texts in the world.

In this spirit, it is the duty of every Hindu to respect every other faith as their own. Whether one prays to Ganesha, Shiva, Jesus, Mother Mary, Lord Buddha, Allah, Kuan Yin or Tua Pek Kong, we see the same one Divine.
